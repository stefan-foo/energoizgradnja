The complete substation or just the transformer

We carry out all types of substation inspections and detailed checks up to and including 35 kV. We inspect the complete substation or just the power transformer, and provide transformer and substation cleaning and maintenance.

We pay particular attention to

  • Power transformer condition
  • Medium-voltage switchgear
  • Low-voltage switchgear
  • Ventilation and cooling
  • Substation building condition

Measurements and testing

We can carry out the following tests and procedures at your site.

  1. Winding resistance
  2. Low-ohm winding resistance
  3. Transformation ratio error and vector group
  4. Short-circuit voltage and no-load current
  5. Core demagnetisation after testing
  6. Transformer oil dielectric breakdown strength
  7. Testing and checking reactive power compensation
  8. Thermal imaging inspection

Calibrated instruments

All instruments and equipment are calibrated at the Nikola Tesla Institute in Belgrade.

  • Capacitance and dielectric loss angle measurement bridge
  • Insulating oil dielectric strength tester
  • Relay protection tester
  • Digital ohmmeter and transformation ratio meter
  • Insulation resistance meter
  • Earth resistance meter
  • Digital ohmmeter
  • Thermal imaging camera
  • Other instruments and equipment

Inspection coordination and documentation

Inspections and detailed checks 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, including non-working days and public holidays.

By prior agreement, we arrange the power outage in good time on the client’s behalf with the local distribution company, submitting the appropriate licences, occupational health and safety (BZR) documentation and other documents.

After a substation inspection and detailed checks, you receive a complete overview of its electrical condition, remedial costs and timing:

  1. Test certificates

    Documentation of the tests performed.

  2. Report on defects

    An overview of the defects identified.

  3. Remedial budget

    Budgeting for rectifying the identified defects.

  4. Time frame

    A time frame for the required work.
