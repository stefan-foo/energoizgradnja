Your transformer under repair.
A replacement at your plant

When collecting your transformer, we deliver and temporarily install our replacement unit while yours is being repaired. This lets you continue using electricity without long periods of downtime.

You can also use a complete replacement substation, which we connect to your incoming supply and loads.

Available ratings

Transformers must be used in accordance with the manufacturer’s recommendations, EPS recommendations, applicable standards and laws.

  • 1000 kVA

  • 2500 kVAVoltage level: 35 kV

Adapting the equipment to your site

We carry out all necessary adaptations when installing the replacement transformer:

  • Adaptation to differences in transformer height.
  • Adaptation of UNP channel sections to differences in wheel centre spacing.
  • Earthing modifications.
  • Other necessary adaptations to site conditions.
  • Where needed, construction of a special ramp on site to move the transformer in or out where height differences or restricted access require it.

Vehicles and field equipment

  • Three self-loading trucks with cranes of different specifications.
  • An aerial work basket for heights up to 21 m.
